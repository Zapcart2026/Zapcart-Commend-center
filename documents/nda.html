Zapcart sources IT hardware on demand from a global supplier network. In the course of discussions the parties will exchange commercially sensitive information and may be introduced to each other's business contacts. Each party agrees as follows.

  1. Purpose. The parties may exchange information to evaluate, quote, source, supply, service or support the purchase or sale of IT hardware, software and related services, and to explore supply or distribution arrangements (the "Purpose").
  2. Confidential Information. All non-public information disclosed by either party, in any form and whether or not marked confidential, including prices, quotations, costs, margins, discount structures, stock positions and availability, part numbers and configurations, customer and end-user identities, supplier and distributor identities, lead times, payment and logistics terms, tender and bid information, technical data, business plans, and the existence and contents of the parties' discussions.
  3. Obligations. The Receiving Party shall: (a) use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose; (b) protect it with at least the care it applies to its own confidential information and in no event less than reasonable care; (c) disclose it only to those of its employees, officers and professional advisers who need it for the Purpose and who are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than these; and (d) remain responsible for any breach by those persons. No Confidential Information may be published, shared with any third party, or used to compete with the Disclosing Party.
  4. Non-Circumvention. This clause is a material condition of the parties' dealings. Where one party ("Introducing Party") introduces the other to any supplier, distributor, manufacturer, customer, end user, agent, financier or freight or logistics provider (an "Introduced Contact"), the other party shall not, for twenty-four (24) months from the date of introduction, directly or indirectly, alone or through any affiliate, employee, agent or nominee:
    1. transact, quote, negotiate or contract with that Introduced Contact in connection with the Purpose or the goods or opportunity to which the introduction related, without the Introducing Party's prior written consent and participation;
    2. bypass, avoid, circumvent or attempt to circumvent the Introducing Party so as to exclude it from any transaction, commission, margin or fee it would otherwise have earned; or
    3. approach that Introduced Contact's parent, subsidiary or affiliate for the same purpose in order to achieve the same effect.
    An Introduced Contact with whom the receiving party can show, by written records predating the introduction, that it already had an established trading relationship is excluded from this clause.
  5. Exclusions. Confidential Information does not include information that is or becomes public other than through breach of this Agreement; was lawfully known to the Receiving Party before disclosure, as shown by its written records; is independently developed without use of the Confidential Information; or is lawfully received from a third party free of any confidentiality obligation. Where disclosure is required by law, regulation, court or regulator, the Receiving Party may disclose only what is required, and shall where lawfully permitted notify the Disclosing Party first so it may seek protective relief.
  6. No Solicitation of Personnel. For twelve (12) months from the Effective Date, neither party shall knowingly solicit for employment any employee of the other who was involved in the Purpose. A general public advertisement not directed at that person is not a breach.
  7. Term and Survival. This Agreement takes effect on the Effective Date and continues for three (3) years. The confidentiality obligations in clause 3 survive for three (3) years after expiry, and indefinitely for any information that constitutes a trade secret under applicable law. The non-circumvention obligation in clause 4 runs for its full 24-month period even if this Agreement has otherwise ended.
  8. Return and Destruction. On written request the Receiving Party shall promptly return or destroy all Confidential Information and copies, and confirm this in writing. It may retain copies required by law, regulation, audit or routine electronic backup, which remain subject to this Agreement for as long as they are retained.
  9. No Licence, No Warranty, No Obligation. No licence or right in any intellectual property is granted. Confidential Information is provided "as is" with no warranty as to accuracy or completeness, and prices and availability are indicative unless confirmed in a signed quotation or purchase order. Nothing here obliges either party to enter into any transaction, and nothing creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or exclusive relationship.
  10. Remedies. The parties agree that damages alone may not be an adequate remedy for breach of clauses 3 or 4, and that the non-breaching party is entitled to seek injunctive or other interim relief in any court of competent jurisdiction, in addition to any other remedy and without the need to post security. Where a party breaches clause 4, the Introducing Party is entitled to recover the gross profit the breaching party derived from the circumvented transaction, in addition to its own documented losses.
  11. Governing Law and Jurisdiction. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Qatar. The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts of Qatar. The parties may instead agree in writing to refer a dispute to arbitration in Doha under the rules of the Qatar International Center for Conciliation and Arbitration, in English, before a sole arbitrator.
  12. General. This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties on its subject matter and supersedes any earlier understanding on it. No variation is effective unless in writing and signed by both parties. A failure or delay in enforcing any right is not a waiver of it. If any provision is held invalid or unenforceable, it shall be limited to the minimum extent necessary and the remainder continues in force. Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other's written consent, except to a successor of its business. This Agreement may be signed in counterparts, and a scanned, electronic or digitally signed copy has the same effect as an original.
This agreement is issued by Zapcart Technology W.L.L. for its commercial dealings. Zapcart recommends that each party take its own legal advice before signing.
